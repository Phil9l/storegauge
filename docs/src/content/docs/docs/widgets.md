---
title: Widgets and menu bar
description: Configure StoreGauge widgets and the menu-bar review count.
---

StoreGauge includes **Performance** and **New Feedback** widgets in medium and large sizes.

- **Performance** can show downloads, proceeds, or both over 7, 14, or 30 days, including movement against the preceding period.
- **New Feedback** can show all reviews needing attention or only 1–2, 1–3, or 4–5 star reviews.

Choose one app in widget configuration, or leave **App** empty to combine the apps selected in StoreGauge. Clicking a widget opens the relevant app, overview, or review inbox.

## Menu bar

The menu-bar gauge shows the number of reviews that need attention. Its popover includes quick actions for the dashboard and review inbox, syncing, copying a seven-day portfolio summary, acknowledging reviews, and opening an individual review’s app.

:::note[Minimal shared data]
The widget extension never receives API credentials or direct database access. StoreGauge writes a small read-only snapshot to its App Group after sync and review actions.
:::
