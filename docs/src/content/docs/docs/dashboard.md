The overview combines selected, non-archived apps from every connected account. Use the chart legend to isolate downloads or updates when both series are available.

## Organize the app list

- **Include or exclude:** Control whether an app contributes to combined dashboard totals.
- **Pin:** Keep important apps above the rest of their account.
- **Archive:** Hide an app and stop syncing it while retaining its local history. Restore it from **Settings → Archived**.
- **Inspect status:** Open an app to see release state, rollout progress, metrics, income, reviews, and recent changes.

## Understand the numbers

StoreGauge keeps labels specific when Apple and Google measure different stages of a funnel or money flow.

- Apple developer proceeds are not merged into Google estimated gross sales or finalized earnings.
- New installs and device updates can be displayed separately.
- Currency values can be converted into one display currency without erasing their original source or category.
- Google crash and ANR rates follow the freshness dates provided by the reporting API.

## Alerts

StoreGauge can notify you about new reviews, state changes, newly discovered apps, rollout completion, and unusual week-over-week movement. Configure notifications in Settings.
